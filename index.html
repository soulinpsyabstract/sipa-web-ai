How it works

Three steps to a live agent

You don't need to write prompts, manage an API key, or touch any code.

01

You tell us about the business

Hours, prices, menu or services, FAQs, policies — whatever you'd tell a new employee on their first day. A short call or a document is enough.

02

We build and test the agent

We set it up on the AI model, load in your business info, and test it against real questions before it goes anywhere near a customer.

03

We deploy it and hand it over

Website script tag, WhatsApp number, or Telegram bot — live on your channel, with a simple way for you to send us updates when your info changes.

What it actually does

What to expect — no exaggeration

An AI agent is genuinely useful for the questions it's built for. It is not magic, and we'd rather tell you the limits up front than have you find them the hard way.

Answers grounded in your own info, not the open internet
Works in Hebrew, English, and Russian
Hands off to a human for anything it shouldn't guess at
Logs conversations so you can see what customers actually ask
Doesn't take payments or place orders on its own
Can still get things wrong — we review and tune it, we don't claim perfection
Doesn't replace a human for anything sensitive (medical, legal, complaints)
Currently website, WhatsApp, Telegram — not Instagram DMs yet
